Question: does the paired comparator effect extend to a recurrent text encoder under the same intervention utility and eligible units?
Candidate: one randomly initialized bidirectional LSTM text encoder, one layer, 384 hidden units per direction, 128-dimensional trainable WordPiece embeddings, vocabulary 30522; packed sequences ignore right padding. No BERT/other pretrained weights or precomputed BERT features are used. Reuse the exact input token IDs, attention mask, WordPiece grouping, AV features/normalization, deterministic 32-D evidence head (main_only), pooling and fusion. Sharing the tokenizer and downstream evidence head limits independence; changed pretraining is also a difference. This tests a non-Transformer text encoder, not a wholly independent multimodal pipeline. No claim of matched predictor capacity or equal semantic reference meaning is made.

Why this choice: retaining the tokenizer avoids changing eligible spans and input length; recurrence provides a structurally different contextual encoder. A T5 encoder was considered but not selected because tokenizer/alignment/replacement adaptation would require additional protocol changes. Only one family is allowed; no search for favorable explanation outcomes. MASK and UNK embeddings may have little/no training exposure; report that limitation and keep both substitutions.

Training: unchanged train/valid arrays; CE + .75 Huber(delta=.5) + .1 auxiliary, deterministic evidence. AdamW recurrent encoder lr=.001, wd=.001; inherited head encoder/fusion lr=.0003/.0005. Batch32, maximum30 epochs, patience8, minimum raw validation MAE with 1e-5 improvement tolerance, clip gradients1. Seeds2026/2027/2028. No test in training. Fixed order MOSI2026 pilot, then remaining jobs only after pilot training/replay review. Maximum six training runs, no family or hyperparameter search. A numerical failure is investigated, not skipped.

Predictor usability: finite predictions, nonconstant predictions, positive validation Pearson correlation and validation MAE below the training-median constant baseline. These are a minimal signal check, not an explanation-success test or performance-matching claim. If pilot fails, stop expansion and record outcome rather than switching candidates. All retained runs are reported; do not discard an unfavorable seed. Smoke results are engineering checks only.
